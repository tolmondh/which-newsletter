About Which Newsletter

This is an independent decision site for creators choosing a newsletter platform. We are not an official partner of beehiiv, Kit, Ghost, or Substack.

What we do

We write plain comparisons and migration guides so you can pick a tool that fits how you work — growth-focused newsletters, course funnels, owned publishing sites, or simple paid writing.

We avoid hype and invented performance stats. When we describe pricing, we talk about structure (what you pay for) and tell you to confirm current numbers on the vendor’s site.

Independence

Content here is editorial. We may add affiliate links later to keep the site free to run. That does not make us an official partner of any platform. If a relationship changes, we’ll say so here and in our affiliate disclosure.

Corrections

Platforms ship features and change pricing often. If something on this site is outdated or wrong, treat vendor docs as source of truth and prefer our framing as a map, not a contract.
