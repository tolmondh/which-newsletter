beehiiv vs Ghost

Both can run a serious newsletter with paid tiers. beehiiv is a hosted newsletter platform built around growth tools and an ad network; Ghost is an open-source publishing and membership platform you can self-host or run on managed Ghost(Pro). This guide is about fit, not hype — verify current plans, fees, and feature gates on each vendor’s site before you commit.

Quick verdict

  • Pick beehiiv if you want the newsletter to grow and earn with as little ops as possible: hosted, referral and boost-style growth tools, an ad network, and paid subscriptions in one dashboard.
  • Pick Ghost if the website and content ownership matter as much as the email: a full publishing CMS, themes you can customize, open-source code, and the option to self-host.
  • Neither is “better” — beehiiv trades some control for growth leverage; Ghost trades some growth tooling for ownership and flexibility.

When beehiiv fits

Choose beehiiv when the inbox issue is the product and you’d rather spend time writing and growing than running infrastructure.

  • You want growth tools inside the platform (referrals, boosts, recommendations) instead of stitching together your own.
  • You want ad revenue without selling every sponsor yourself, alongside paid subscriptions.
  • You don’t want to think about servers, theme code, or email delivery setup.
  • You care more about subscriber growth and monetization than a highly customized website.

When Ghost fits

Stay on (or choose) Ghost when the publication is a website-first media business and ownership is a top priority.

  • You want a real CMS: custom themes, pages, tags, and SEO-driven articles that stand on their own as a site.
  • You value open source and the option to self-host, including control over your data and stack.
  • Your revenue model is paid memberships you run through Stripe and sponsors you sell directly.
  • You or your team are comfortable with theme customization or server maintenance (if self-hosting).

Honest flip side: Ghost’s flexibility means more of the growth work — referrals, cross-promotion, ad sales — is yours to build or buy.

Pricing shape (not a rate card)

Don’t trust any roundup’s numbers, including ours. Think in shape, then verify:

  • beehiiv: Free Launch tier to start; paid Scale/Max tiers unlock higher limits and advanced growth and monetization features. See our beehiiv pricing guide.
  • Ghost(Pro): Paid managed hosting tiered by member count and features. Self-hosted Ghost is free software, but you pay for the server, email sending, and your own time.
  • Paid subscriptions on either: Stripe processing fees apply. Check each platform’s current docs for any platform-side cut on paid revenue — it changes the math at scale.

Run the numbers for your member count and expected paid conversions on both current pricing pages before choosing.
