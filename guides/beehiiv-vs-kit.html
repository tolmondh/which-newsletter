beehiiv vs Kit

Both own the “creator email” conversation — but they’re different products. beehiiv is a newsletter growth and monetization platform; Kit (formerly ConvertKit) is a creator ESP built for funnels and commerce.

Quick verdict

  • Pick beehiiv if the newsletter is the product: publish, grow the list, sell ads/sponsorships, and optionally paid subscriptions.
  • Pick Kit if email is a funnel: tags, visual automations, launches, and selling courses or digital products.
  • Neither is “better” — wrong tool costs you either growth leverage or launch leverage.

When beehiiv fits

  • You publish on a cadence and care about open web + email as one publication.
  • Sponsorships or an ad network are part of the revenue plan — not an afterthought.
  • You want paid subscriptions with a software fee model rather than a heavy creator-commerce stack.
  • You’re leaving Substack for growth/monetization tools (see beehiiv vs Substack).

When Kit fits

  • Your newsletter is top-of-funnel for courses, cohorts, or digital products.
  • You live in tags, segments, and multi-step automations.
  • You need “bought / didn’t buy / abandoned cart” style logic more than a media ad stack.
  • You’re fine pairing Kit with a separate site or course host.

When people use both

Some teams run beehiiv as the public newsletter and keep Kit (or another ESP) for launch sequences. That’s valid — and heavier. Only do it if one tool clearly can’t cover a must-have workflow. Most solo creators should pick one primary home for the list.

What to do next

  1. Write your primary revenue line Ads/sponsorships + paid newsletter → lean beehiiv. Course/product launches → lean Kit.
  2. List three must-have workflows If two are “ad insert + referral growth + paid tier,” beehiiv. If two are “tag buyers + launch sequence + evergreen cart,” Kit.
  3. Trial the primary tool with a real send Import a small segment; don’t migrate the whole business on day one.

If your intent is a growth-and-ads newsletter (not a course ESP), beehiiv is the clearer fit — start free on Launch and upgrade when monetization needs it.
