beehiiv vs Substack

You’re not shopping for writing tips — you’re deciding whether to stay on Substack or move to a growth-first newsletter stack. Here’s the head-to-head that matters if you’re close to switching.

Quick verdict

  • Choose beehiiv if the newsletter is a media product: list growth, referrals, sponsorships/ads, and keeping more of paid subscription revenue matter more than Substack’s discovery network.
  • Stay on Substack if simplicity, network recommendations, and “just write + charge readers” still beat ops and tooling.
  • Don’t switch for vibes. Switch when a missing feature (ads tooling, branding control, lower take-rate on paid) is costing you real money or time.

When beehiiv wins

beehiiv is built for operators who treat the list as an asset to grow and monetize — not only as a writing home.

  • Growth tooling — referrals, recommendations/boosts, and analytics aimed at list growth.
  • Ads & sponsorship paths — built-in ad network and sponsorship-style workflows vs mostly DIY on Substack.
  • Paid subscriptions without a platform cut — beehiiv positions itself as taking 0% of paid subscription revenue (you still pay Stripe processing). Substack typically takes a percentage of paid revenue. Model both before you move.
  • Brand control as you scale — custom domain / remove branding options on paid tiers; Substack’s product surface is still “you’re on Substack.”
  • Unlimited sends on plan — you’re not metering sends the way some ESPs do; confirm current plan limits on beehiiv’s pricing page.

When Substack still wins

  • Discovery — recommendations and the Substack app still help writers find readers without a separate growth stack.
  • Lowest ops — write, publish, charge. No plan ladder to decode if you’re early and paid writing is the whole business.
  • Audience already lives there — if most of your readers found you via Substack and you don’t need ads tooling, migration friction can outweigh feature gains.
  • You’re not monetizing beyond reader-paid — if sponsorships aren’t on the roadmap, beehiiv’s growth/ad strengths matter less.

Run a simple model: expected paid revenue × take-rate vs beehiiv plan cost at your list size. That’s the switch math — not vibes.

Ops & product shape

  • Substack = writer UX + network. Weak as a marketing automation platform.
  • beehiiv = newsletter OS with growth/monetization. More to configure; more leverage if you use it.
  • Migration is real work — especially paid subscribers. Billing does not teleport. See our migration outline.

If you’re ready to switch

  1. Confirm the money case Paid take-rate, sponsorship plans, and beehiiv tier at your subscriber count.
  2. Open a beehiiv account on Launch (free) Import a test segment, authenticate sending, and send to yourself before a full cutover.
  3. Plan paid carefully Communicate early; expect some churn; don’t dual-bill readers.
  4. Cut over with a clear “you’re here now” issue Update bios and site CTAs after the first clean send.

If growth tools, ads, and keeping paid revenue matter more than Substack’s network, start on beehiiv and validate with a small import — not a leap of faith.
