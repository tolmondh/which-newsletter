Looking past Substack usually means you want lower take-rate on paid, better growth/ad tooling, stronger automations, or a real owned site. Here’s a short, honest shortlist — not a bloated “50 tools” roundup.

Why people leave Substack

  • Paid revenue share starts to sting as subscriptions grow.
  • They want sponsorship/ad workflows, not only reader-paid.
  • They need more branding control or a less “you’re on Substack” feel.
  • They need ESP-style tagging and automations Substack doesn’t prioritize.
  • They want a full publication website they control (Ghost territory).

1. beehiiv — best default alternative for newsletter operators

If Substack felt like a writing home but you’re building a media-style newsletter (growth + ads + optional paid), beehiiv is the alternative to evaluate first.

Why it fits leavers:

  • Growth tooling (referrals, boosts) aimed at the list.
  • Ad network / sponsorship paths built in.
  • Paid subscriptions with a 0% platform-cut positioning (Stripe fees still apply) — compare carefully to Substack’s take-rate.
  • Free Launch tier to test before a full migration.

Honest caveats: you give up Substack’s network discovery; paid-subscriber migration takes work; paid beehiiv tiers cost software money as you scale. Read beehiiv vs Substack and the migration outline.

2. Kit — best if Substack failed you on funnels

Choose Kit when the real pain isn’t “newsletter ads” — it’s launches, tags, and selling products from email. Kit won’t replace Substack’s discovery network; it replaces the missing ESP.

Fits when: courses, digital products, and visual automations are the point.

3. Ghost — best if you want an owned publication site

Ghost is for publishers who want a real CMS, memberships via Stripe, and long-term ownership (Ghost(Pro) or self-host). Growth and ads are more DIY; there’s no Substack-style network.

Fits when: brand site + members matter more than built-in newsletter growth hacks.

When staying on Substack is smarter

  • Network discovery is still your main growth channel.
  • You’re early, paid writing is simple, and ops overhead would slow you down.
  • You don’t have a clear monetization path that beehiiv/Kit/Ghost unlock.

Leaving without a reason is just expensive busywork.

Pick in one minute

  • Growth + ads + paid newsletter business → beehiiv
  • Course/product email funnels → Kit
  • Owned magazine site + memberships → Ghost
  • Simple paid writing + network → stay on Substack
