Privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026.

Which Newsletter is a small static website run by Tolmond Harrison. It has no user accounts, no comment section, and no sign-up forms.

What this site collects

This site does not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, or payment details. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub processes standard technical data (such as IP addresses in server logs) to deliver pages. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Analytics

I use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count page views (for example, which pages are read and roughly where visitors come from). GoatCounter does not set cookies, does not build personal profiles, and does not track you across other websites. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

Cookies

This site itself does not set cookies. If you click an affiliate link (for example to beehiiv), the site you land on may set its own cookies to credit the referral. Those cookies are controlled by that company, not by me.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are referral links, including links to beehiiv. If you sign up through them, I may earn a commission at no cost to you. See the affiliate disclosure.

Third-party sites

Links go to other websites with their own privacy policies. I don't control those sites and am not responsible for how they handle your data.

I don't sell your data

I don't sell, rent, or share personal information, because this site doesn't collect any.

Children

This site is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
